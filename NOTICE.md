## Quran.com

Word-by-word English glosses, transliterations, the Uthmani word text, explicit QPC page/line
fields, and the Saheeh International verse translation are obtained from the
[Quran.com API](https://quran.com). Quran.com is an open-source project. This reader is not
endorsed by or affiliated with Quran.com. The Saheeh International translation remains the
copyright of its rights holders and is used with attribution.

## QUL 15-line Madani layouts

The 15-line page structure and line types (sūrah headings, basmalas, ayah lines) are based on
the [Quranic Universal Library 15-line Madani layout](https://github.com/blueheron786/quranic-universal-library-mushaf-layouts)
dataset, which describes the 604-page Madanī print. Layout facts are used to render the
mushaf page grid.

## What was changed

- Morphology, lemmas, roots and features were reformatted from the corpus text file into
  JSON shards. The annotations themselves were not altered.
- Qur'anic text is displayed verbatim; page and line placement follows the documented
  15-line layout and Quran.com's explicit page/line fields.
- Grammar explanations, root "core senses" and occurrence groupings are generated by this
  app from the underlying data. They are aids for learning, not a tafsīr.
